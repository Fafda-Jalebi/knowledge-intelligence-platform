# Bridge Inspection Manual - Scour and Fatigue

## 1 Scour Countermeasures

Riprap aprons and articulated concrete blocks are the usual scour countermeasures at bridge piers. Inspection intervals shorten after any flood exceeding the design discharge.

Riprap sizing follows HEC-18 guidelines: median stone diameter based on approach velocity, flow depth, and pier geometry. Filter fabric required beneath riprap to prevent piping.

Articulated concrete blocks (ACBs) provide flexible armor that conforms to scour hole geometry. Cabled blocks preferred for high-velocity sites.

## 2 Fatigue in Welded Details

Fatigue cracking initiates at welded attachment details subject to distortion induced stress. Crack growth is monitored against the detail category assigned during design.

Common fatigue-prone details:
- Category E: Cover plate ends, weld toes
- Category E': Stiffener weld toes
- Category D: Groove welds with backing bars

Inspection methods:
- Visual (VT) for surface cracks
- Magnetic particle (MT) for near-surface
- Ultrasonic (UT) for internal flaws
- Eddy current (ET) for non-ferrous

Retrofit options:
- Peening of weld toes
- Bolted cover plates (remove weld)
- Drilled crack tip arrest holes
- Composite reinforcement (CFRP)
