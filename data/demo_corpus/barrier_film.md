# Barrier Film Technical Note

## 1 Multilayer Film Structure

Multilayer films combine a moisture barrier with an oxygen barrier because no single economical polymer provides both. Oxygen transmission rate is quoted per square metre per day.

Typical structure: PET / adhesive / EVOH / adhesive / PE
- PET: Mechanical strength, printability
- EVOH: Excellent oxygen barrier (requires dry conditions)
- PE: Heat seal layer, moisture barrier

## 2 Modified Atmosphere Packaging

Replacing headspace air with a nitrogen and carbon dioxide mixture slows oxidative rancidity in fatty snack products. Typical gas mix: 70% N2, 30% CO2. CO2 dissolves in product water phase, lowering pH slightly and inhibiting microbial growth.

Film permeability must match product respiration rate. Too low -> pack collapse; too high -> insufficient atmosphere modification.

## 3 Shelf Life Prediction

Shelf life is determined by the first quality attribute to reach its limit:
- Oxidative rancidity (peroxide value)
- Moisture gain/loss (texture change)
- Microbial growth (water activity)
- Vitamin degradation

Accelerated shelf life testing at elevated temperatures uses Arrhenius modeling to predict ambient shelf life.
